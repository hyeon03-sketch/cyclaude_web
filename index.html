Common Questions

How do I apply for my ARC (Alien Resident Certificate)?
  1. Prepare your passport, one photo, and your enrollment certificate.
  2. Visit the household registration office within 15 days of arrival.
  3. Pay the fee if required, then collect your ARC.
＋ Add reminder to calendar
How do I get health insurance (NHI)?
  1. For your first 6 months, you're covered by the school's group insurance.
  2. After 6 months, apply for National Health Insurance (NHI) at a district office.
  3. Bring your ARC and enrollment certificate.
How do I register for Fall courses?
  1. Check the add/drop period dates on the Notice board above.
  2. Log in to the course registration system with your student ID.
  3. Select your courses and confirm before the deadline.
